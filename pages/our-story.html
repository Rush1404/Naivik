Who is Naivik

Our Story

Naivik was founded in Toronto in 2015 as a small consulting shop. What began as hands-on advisory work grew, by necessity, into embedded talent placement and full enterprise solution delivery — because the problems our clients brought us rarely stayed contained to one discipline.

That range shows in the work itself. We've been the boots on the ground for early-stage startups resetting an operating model before they'd found product-market fit. We've helped a consumer hardware company get a product off the ground after discovering their architecture and processes — not the product — were what had stalled delivery. We've supported a global satellite operator's shift from a bespoke GEO platform to LEO, built around an entirely new service and support model. We've built and operated compensation engines for Tier 1 telcos whose existing platforms couldn't scale into new lines of business. And we led the first end-to-end migration of a Tier 1 telco's billing platform to cloud-native infrastructure — 40 points of integration, a million subscribers, and not a single customer-facing outage, a first in that BSS's history.

Along the way, we've also placed dozens of hard-to-fill product and project management roles — proof that the same judgment that shapes a delivery plan also shapes who belongs in the room to execute it.
