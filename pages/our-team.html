Who's behind Naivik

Leadership with direct operating experience

Prospective clients and candidates routinely research our team before engaging. The following reflects what they will find.

Keith Maharaj

President & CEO

Keith brings a product leader's discipline to Naivik's delivery work. Before co-founding Naivik, he held Director-level product management roles at TELUS, including leading the relaunch of Public Mobile. He also designed and ran a multi-vendor environment for a $300M business — structured so vendors couldn't point fingers at each other, only cooperate — and led the first end-to-end migration of a Tier 1 telco's billing platform to cloud-native infrastructure, with zero customer-facing outages across a million subscribers. That experience — owning outcomes across vendors, platforms, and teams that each have their own incentives — shapes how Naivik scopes every engagement: against the business case it's meant to serve, not just the spec it's meant to meet.

Manoj Jayaswal

CTO

Manoj has spent nearly three decades as a solution architect across telecommunications and banking, with deep experience in BSS/OSS environments, cloud migrations, and enterprise architecture at scale. He served as technical lead for the data migration at the center of Naivik's largest engagement — a Tier 1 telco's cloud-native billing platform migration spanning forty points of integration, with zero customer-facing outages. That same rigor defines how he approaches every system Naivik touches: understand it fully before you change it.
